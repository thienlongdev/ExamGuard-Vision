## 2. Terminology & Entity Disambiguation

In several places in Stage 1 reports, raw bounding box detections across independent video frames were loosely termed "students" or "detected students".

To maintain scientific rigor:
- **Frame**: An individual image timestamp from an exam room camera.
- **Bounding Box / Behavior Detection**: A single detection candidate `[x1, y1, x2, y2, class_id, conf]` in a single frame. A single student appearing in 100 frames generates 100 detections.
- **Tracked Student / Unique Student**: A physical person whose identity is linked across multiple frames via a tracking algorithm (e.g. ByteTrack) with an assigned persistent `track_id`.

Because the qualitative zero-shot evaluation on CCTV Exam Monitor ran frame-by-frame detector inference without ByteTrack tracker state, these counts represent **raw behavior box detections**, NOT unique students. Both reports have been updated to explicitly label these counts as **raw detections / behavior boxes**.

## 4. Inaccurate Precision Characterization for `turn_head`

### Issue
Section 10 of `reports/STAGE1_V3_TRAINING_FINAL.md` stated:
> *"turn_head & discuss: Both classes achieved high precision..."*

### Verification Against Stage 1 Baseline Metrics
- `turn_head` Validation Precision: **0.2080**
- `turn_head` Test Precision: **0.4855**
- `turn_head` Test Recall: **0.1509**
- `turn_head` Test mAP50: **0.1612**

A precision of 0.4855 (and validation precision of 0.2080) cannot be characterized as "high precision". In reality, `turn_head` suffers from severe low recall (0.1509) and modest precision (0.4855), making it one of the two core bottleneck classes alongside `head_down` (recall 0.0666).

### Correction
Section 10 has been amended to accurately state:
> *"turn_head exhibited substantial bottlenecking (validation precision 0.2080, recall 0.1749; test precision 0.4855, recall 0.1509), suffering from high false negative rates and confusion with normal writing posture. discuss achieved moderate-high precision (0.6454 test) and good recall (0.8137 test)."*
